## Notes on filling it in

- Every factual claim in Part 1 gets a source and a date, inline or in a short sources list at the end of the part.
- In Part 2, write "(inferred)" after any name or reporting line you did not confirm. Prep documents will copy this section, and an inference written as fact gets repeated in the room.
- The recruiter briefing is written as the recruiter would write it to themselves, in the company's vocabulary, not the candidate's.
- For multi-product companies, add a short table in Part 1 listing every product line: name, one sentence, who uses it.
