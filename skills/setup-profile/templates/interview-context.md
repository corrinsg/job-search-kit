# Interview context

Your own answers, in your own words, to the questions that come up in every process. Prep documents tailor only the company-specific questions (tell me about yourself, why looking, why this role, what do you know about us, salary, notice). The rest are drawn from here, so write them once and well.

Write in first person, as you would say them. Bullets of key beats are better than paragraphs.
