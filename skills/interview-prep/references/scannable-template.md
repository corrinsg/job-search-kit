## Notes on filling it

- The interviewer sections do the heaviest lifting. If research found little, say so in one line rather than padding with inferences.
- "Strongest angles" and the Q3 answer are different things: angles are why they should want the candidate; Q3 is why the candidate wants them.
- Scenario answers are beats, three to five lines. If the candidate asks for a full mock answer later, write it in a separate section or file.
- Interview-only facts from `role-context.md` are allowed in beats. Never-claim items are not.
