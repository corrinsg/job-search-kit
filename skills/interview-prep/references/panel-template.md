# Panel and onsite template

For several rounds in one day or one week. Each round gets a section built like a trimmed scannable prep; the STAR bank lives in a sibling `interview-qa.md` (see `star-format.md`) so examples are written once and referenced from each round. Mark rounds DONE as they finish and add the live intel; the document is a living brief across the loop.

```
# <Company>: <Role> | Panel prep

**Loop:** <N rounds, dates> | **Fit:** <X/10> | **STAR bank:** interview-qa.md

## The role in a nutshell
<Three to five bullets from analysis.md: the business problem, the scope, the outcome expected, the risks they want to avoid in this hire.>

## Live intel from the rounds so far
- <Date, round, what was asked, what the interviewer reacted to, anything they said about the team or the decision. Append after every round.>

## The through-line across all rounds
<One paragraph: the single story about the candidate that every round should reinforce, and the three patterns from profile/lessons.md to land in every answer.>

## Round 1 (<duration>) | <Competency, e.g. Case study>  <✅ DONE once finished>
### <Interviewer name>, <Title>
- <Background bullets, confirmed or inferred>
- **They'll probe for:** ...
- **Surface for them:** ...
### <Second interviewer>
### How to win this round
- <Format expectations, from recruiter intel or the company's public interview guide>
- <The structure to drive: see case-study-and-system-design.md for case and design rounds>
- <The two or three STAR examples from interview-qa.md most likely to be needed, by name>
- <Compliance or domain constraints to raise unprompted, if the industry is regulated>
### Questions to ask (2, do not repeat across rounds)
- *"..."*
- *"..."*

## Round 2 (<duration>) | <Competency>
<Same shape.>

## Round N ...

## Panel-wide checklist
- [ ] Each round's two questions are different
- [ ] The through-line appears in every Q1-style opener
- [ ] Tradeoff named in every project story
- [ ] Case and design rounds end with a committed recommendation
- [ ] Logistics: <building, link, breaks, water>
- [ ] After each round: add live intel above and note which STAR examples were used
```

## Notes

- Interviewers in a loop compare notes. Reusing the same example in two rounds is fine if it is the strongest fit; reusing the same question for them is not.
- Allocate examples across rounds in advance, in a small table at the top of `interview-qa.md`, so the candidate is not reaching for a story under pressure.
- Shared ground between rounds is common (two rounds both touching execution). Decide in advance which angle each round gets.
- For a multi-product company, every product line should be covered somewhere in the loop prep, with interviewer titles as the guide to which round will raise which.
