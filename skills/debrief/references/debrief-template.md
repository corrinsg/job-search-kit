# Debrief template

```
# <Company>: <Role> | Debrief

**Source:** <candidate's own read | recruiter feedback call on <date> | written feedback | interviewer comment in the room>
**Outcome:** <progressing | awaiting | rejected | offer | withdrew>
**Process timing:** <dates of each round, gaps between them, anything unusual about pacing>

## Per-interview feedback
### Round 1 | <Competency> with <Interviewer(s)>
**What was asked:** <questions as remembered>
**Positives:** <what landed, in the interviewer's words where available>
**Gaps:** <what was thin, missed, or misread>

### Round 2 | ...

## Cross-cutting themes (ranked by frequency)
1. <Theme that appeared in N rounds, with the evidence>
2. ...

## Candidate's read versus the feedback
<Where they agree, where they differ. A strength the candidate felt and the feedback did not mention is worth noting; so is a gap the candidate did not feel.>

## What did not appear in the feedback
<Strengths that went unremarked; concerns the candidate expected that never came.>

## What to do with this for future <sector or role type> interviews
- <Concrete behaviours for the next prep, each pointing to the lesson appended to profile/lessons.md>

## Lessons appended to profile/lessons.md
- <YYYY-MM-DD | title>
```

For a debrief after a recruiter screen, collapse to: what the recruiter asked, the band disclosed, the next steps, and one line on whether anything needs to change before the hiring manager call.
