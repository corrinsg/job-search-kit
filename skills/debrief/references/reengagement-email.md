# Post-rejection re-engagement email

Hiring managers remember candidates who lost gracefully and said something specific. A short note within a week of the rejection keeps the relationship warm for the next opening, which at growing companies is often months, not years, away.

```
# <Company> | Re-engagement note to <name>, <date>

## Strategic angle
<One or two lines: why this company is worth keeping warm, which team or role type to signal interest in, and what the candidate learned from the process that they can mention credibly.>

## The email
Subject: Thank you, and staying in touch

Hi <first name>,

Thank you for the time you and the team put into the process. I enjoyed the <specific round or conversation>, and <one specific thing learned or appreciated, in one sentence>.

I would be glad to be considered for future roles on <team or area>. If something opens up in the next few months, I would welcome a conversation.

Best,
<Candidate name>

## Tone notes
- Four sentences. Warmth without neediness.
- One specific detail from the process; it proves the note is not a template.
- No request for feedback here (that is the feedback call), no reference to the decision being wrong.

## If they reply positively
- Thank them, confirm how they prefer to stay in touch, and set a reminder for three months. Add a `follow-up` timeline event to tracker.json with the date.

## If no reply
- Nothing further. Add a one-line note in tracker.json. Revisit only when a relevant role appears, and open that message by referring to this one.
```
