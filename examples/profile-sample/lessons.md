# Lessons

Dated, generalised rules appended by the debrief skill after each interview, rejection, or recruiter comment. Every prep reads this file and turns the most recent and most repeated lessons into "patterns to land in every answer".

---

## 2026-01-22 | Close every case study with a committed recommendation
**Rule:** End any case or open-ended product discussion with "my recommendation is A, I would validate it by B, and revise if C". Time-box discovery to 15% of the slot.
**Why:** A fleet-software case study at a Series B company. Feedback: good questions, strong framing, "but we never heard a plan". Ran out of time still weighing two options.
**How to apply:** Case study rounds, "how would you approach" questions in hiring-manager interviews, and the first-90-days question. Write the closing sentence on the pre-call checklist.
**Evidence:** The solver-versus-workflow decision at Freightline is a committed call with a named tradeoff; lead with it.

## 2026-02-09 | Name the cost, not just the win, for engineering panels
**Rule:** In every project story told to an engineering leader, say what was given up and when the decision would have gone the other way.
**Why:** Engineering manager round at a field-service company. Self-read: told the dispatch rebuild as a win story; the follow-up was "what did that cost you?" and the answer came late.
**How to apply:** Pre-load one tradeoff into the "tell me about yourself" answer; end the challenge story with the cost line.
**Evidence:** All three employer blocks in role-context.md have a tradeoff story ready.
