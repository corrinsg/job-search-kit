## Section rules

- Section order is fixed: Summary, Side projects (if any), Experience, Speaking and writing (if preferences include it), Skills, Volunteering (if included), Education. Side projects sit above Experience when the role values building; move them below Experience for conservative employers.
- Omit optional sections rather than leaving them empty.
- Dates use en dashes: "January 2021 – March 2024". The most recent role uses "Present" only if `role-context.md` says it is current.
- Headings use the pipe as the separator. No long dashes anywhere.
- The Tailoring Notes section is for the candidate and the next tailoring run. It never goes to an employer; the candidate copies everything above the horizontal rule into their CV layout.
